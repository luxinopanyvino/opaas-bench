Our values

Five principles that become concrete engineering decisions, and that you can check in what we publish.

Governance

01

An AI's decisions have an owner and a trail.

  • Hash-chained, sealed records
  • Policies with comply, violate or undecidable verdicts
  • Doubtful actions held for human approval
  • Thresholds are set by the operator, never by the tool

Sovereignty

02

Your data, your models, your infrastructure.

  • Works with no network connection (air-gapped)
  • No mandatory dependency on the cloud
  • Certificates verified with no server and no network
  • Open source: no black boxes

Security

03

Secure by design, minimal by default.

  • Ed25519 signatures over canonical JSON (RFC 8785)
  • Minimised evidence: fingerprints, never messages or secrets
  • Datasets and auxiliary models pinned by hash
  • Prompt-injection scenarios with canaries

Identity

04

Every component knows what it is, and can prove it.

  • Deployment fingerprint: model, quantisation, engine and hardware
  • Identification of agents, models and tools
  • Actor chain: which agent acted on whose behalf
  • Issuers with DIDs and W3C verifiable credentials

Explainability

05

Results you can explain and repeat.

  • Results per category, never an overall grade
  • Deterministic detection, no “LLM as a judge”
  • What was not measured is declared; zero means “measured, and it was zero”
  • Methodology and decisions published, with their cost

OPAAS

On-Prem AI Audit Suite

Evaluates an AI deployment that is already running —without deploying or downloading the model— and turns the result into a signed digital certificate a third party can verify offline.

  • Model performance, capability, consistency, safety and compliance.
  • Agent auditability with instrumented ground truth, not an LLM judge.
  • Whole-system audit: models, agents, tools and records.
  • W3C credentials signed with Ed25519, and continuous change watch.

What OPAAS evaluates in a model

Performance

A
  • Tokens/s and TTFT
  • Latency under concurrency
  • VRAM, power and hardware fingerprint

Capability

B
  • Instruction following (IFEval)
  • Answering from context (SQuAD 2.0)
  • Abstaining when it does not know

Consistency

C
  • Determinism across repetitions
  • Stability under concurrent load
  • Robustness to malformed input and truncated context

Safety

D
  • Over-refusal (XSTest)
  • Bias per social category (BBQ)
  • Toxicity under provocation (RTP)

Compliance

E
  • Requirements cited to the clause
  • Declared and verified, always apart
  • What is unsupported is marked as such

And beyond the model: seven audit modes

  • benchCertifies a model in its real deployment.
  • discoverInventories engines, agents and tools.
  • smithScenarios with mock tools as ground truth.
  • swarmDeclared against observed multi-agent flows.
  • sentinelA proxy that observes and holds actions for a human.
  • inquestPost-hoc audit from records.
  • auditThe whole system, with a single certificate.

Evolve

No-code · low-code · air-gapped · self-sufficient

A platform to adapt, orchestrate, evaluate and deploy multimodal artificial intelligence locally —vision, speech, SLMs, LLMs, agents, RAG and graphs— on on-premise hardware and edge devices, without relying on external services to run.

  • Visual flow design for those who do not code; extensible with code for those who do.
  • The full cycle inside your network: from tuning the model to deploying and evaluating it.
  • From the GPU server to the edge device, with the same flow.
  • Self-sufficient: no cloud licences and no external calls at run time.

Evolve is in active development. We will publish the repository once its first version is usable.
